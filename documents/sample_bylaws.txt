Riverside Garden Club — Sample Bylaws

Article 1 — Dues. Annual membership dues are $25 per household, payable by
March 1 each year. Members joining after September 1 pay half dues for the
remainder of the year.

Article 2 — Meetings. The club meets the second Tuesday of every month at
the community center, 7:00 PM. A quorum for any vote is ten members in
good standing.

Article 3 — Garden plot fees. Plot fees are $40 per season. Plots not
planted by June 1 may be reassigned to the waiting list after one warning
email.

Article 4 — Officers. The board consists of a President, Vice President,
Treasurer, and Secretary, elected each October for one-year terms.
