RIVERSIDE GARDEN CLUB — DUES POLICY (officer reference)

AMOUNTS
- Annual membership dues: $25 per household, payable by March 1 each year.
- Members joining after September 1 pay half dues ($12.50) for the rest of the year.
- Garden plot fees (separate): $40 per season, due April 1.

HOW MEMBERS PAY
- Cash or check at any monthly meeting (treasurer issues a receipt on the spot)
- Check by mail: Riverside Garden Club, PO Box 214
- (online payments: not available yet)

WHEN A HOUSEHOLD IS BEHIND
- March 15: friendly reminder email from the treasurer
- April 1: second reminder; plot renewals pause until dues are current
- April 15: membership lapses; rejoining requires full-year dues again

WHAT THE BOT MAY DO
- The dues follow-up automation DRAFTS reminder messages for the treasurer
  to review and send. It never messages members by itself.
